# CropCare AI

## Problem Statement
Farmers often struggle to identify plant diseases accurately and timely, leading to significant crop loss. Traditional methods of diagnosis are slow and require expert intervention, which is not always accessible.

## Project Description
CropCare AI is a precision diagnostics tool that uses Google's Gemini AI to analyze plant images and provide instant, localized treatment plans. It integrates real-time weather data to offer context-aware advice and features a community forum for farmers to share knowledge.

## Google AI Usage
### Tools / Models Used
- Gemini 3 Flash (`gemini-3-flash-preview`)

### How Google AI Was Used
The Gemini 3 Flash model is used to analyze uploaded images of crops. It identifies the plant species, detects diseases or pests, assesses severity, and recommends both organic and chemical treatment protocols.
